# CREDITspective Theme Guide

The original teal palette is credible, but it is not automatically the strongest option. Teal is widely used in fintech, risk, health, and ESG software, so it can feel familiar rather than distinctive.

## Recommended default: Midnight Cobalt

**Why it is the strongest starting point**
- Signals institutional trust and enterprise software maturity.
- Feels more AI-forward than the original teal direction.
- Creates stronger contrast for product screenshots and evidence panels.
- Avoids looking like a generic ESG or compliance platform.

## Alternatives

### Institutional Teal
Calm, credible, and modern. Best when the priority is approachability and continuity with the current design.

### Forest & Gold
Established, patient-capital, and premium. Best when the brand should feel closer to an asset manager or private-markets institution.

### Graphite & Copper
Distinctive, editorial, and sophisticated. Best when the goal is to stand apart from blue and teal enterprise-software competitors.

## How to use the selector

Open `standalone.html` or `index.html`. The floating theme-review control in the lower-right corner switches the full site between all four palettes and remembers the selection in the browser.

The selector is intended only for design review. Remove it from the production website after choosing a direction.
